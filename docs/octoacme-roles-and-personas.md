# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors champion the initiative, ensure it has organizational support, and make business-level decisions that are outside the delivery team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and provide sponsorship
- Help secure organizational resources and resolve priority conflicts
- Make or escalate business decisions when trade-offs exceed the team's authority

### Collaboration
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority
- Receive progress, risk, and decision updates from the Project Manager
- Work with stakeholders to resolve organizational blockers without taking over day-to-day delivery

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative remains supported and aligned with business objectives
- Enable timely decisions on escalated scope, priority, or resources

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and sponsor updates
- Escalations and decision requests from the Project Manager or Product Manager

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs and business processes into clear, actionable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and constraints
- Help define clear, testable requirements and acceptance criteria
- Identify requirement gaps, assumptions, and dependencies

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager and stakeholders to refine needs and backlog items
- Clarify requirements with Developers during implementation
- Partner with QA/Testing to ensure acceptance criteria cover intended business behavior
- Coordinate with the Project Manager when requirement changes affect scope or schedule

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity and rework by making business needs understandable and verifiable
- Keep requirements traceable to stakeholder needs and intended outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops and process documentation
- Backlog refinement and acceptance-criteria reviews

---

## UX/Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX/Product Designers research user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and map key journeys or workflows
- Create and validate interaction designs and prototypes
- Document usability and accessibility considerations

### Collaboration
- Partner with the Product Manager to align experience choices with user and product outcomes
- Work with Developers and the Technical Lead to assess feasibility and implementation details
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility acceptance
- Share research and design decisions with stakeholders and the Project Manager when they affect scope or timing

### Goals
- Deliver experiences that are useful, understandable, and accessible
- Identify usability risks early enough to inform planning and implementation

### Typical Communication
- User research and design reviews
- Design specifications, prototypes, and usability findings

---

## Technical Leads/Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound design and integration decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical designs, architecture, and integration approaches
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support engineering estimates and maintainability and testability practices

### Collaboration
- Guide Developers through design choices while leaving implementation ownership with the team
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on technical dependencies, risks, and estimates
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure designs are testable
- Consult Security/Privacy Leads and Release/Operations Leads on relevant design and operational concerns

### Goals
- Enable reliable, maintainable solutions that meet requirements
- Surface technical trade-offs and risks early

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical decision records
- Engineering planning and dependency discussions

---

## Security/Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security/Privacy Leads advise on security and privacy requirements, risks, and controls relevant to the initiative.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and threats
- Recommend controls and review relevant designs or data handling
- Help define security/privacy verification and incident escalation needs

### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager and stakeholders to clarify relevant obligations and user expectations
- Advise Developers and Technical Leads on secure design and implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy verification
- Record risks and mitigations with the Project Manager and escalate significant concerns through established channels

### Goals
- Address security and privacy risks throughout delivery rather than only at release
- Make required controls and verification responsibilities clear

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and design consultations
- Security/privacy requirements and review findings

---

## Release/Operations Leads (SRE)

### Role Summary
Release/Operations Leads (including Site Reliability Engineers where applicable) prepare services and teams for safe deployment and reliable operation.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, deployment steps, and rollback plans
- Establish monitoring, alerting, and service reliability expectations
- Coordinate release verification and operational handoff

### Collaboration
- Coordinate deployment timing and operational risks with the Project Manager
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads on deployability, observability, and remediation
- Partner with QA/Testing on smoke tests and release verification
- Align with stakeholders on service-impact communications and with the Product Manager on release outcomes

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and support reliable service operation
- Ensure teams can detect and respond to issues after deployment

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment plans
- Operational dashboards, incident updates, and handoff documentation

---

## Data/Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Leads help teams define and validate the data needed to measure product outcomes and operational performance.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into instrumentation and reporting needs
- Define data quality, event tracking, and analysis requirements
- Help interpret results and identify measurement limitations

### Collaboration
- Align success metrics and analysis with the Product Manager and stakeholders
- Work with Developers on instrumentation and data pipelines
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate tracking and data quality
- Coordinate measurement dependencies and risks with the Project Manager

### Goals
- Make outcome measurement reliable and actionable
- Help teams use evidence to evaluate releases and guide iteration

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and analytics plans
- Dashboards, data quality findings, and outcome reviews

---

These roles may be combined or assigned only when needed, depending on project size, complexity, and risk. Teams should make ownership explicit when a role is shared or not separately staffed.
