# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme moves work through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Initiation aligns the team on the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, risks, resources, and timeline. Once priority and team availability are confirmed, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, a Definition of Done, and milestones.

During execution, teams deliver small increments and track work on a project board. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager owns outcomes, prioritization, and success measures. Developers build and test against acceptance criteria, QA validates quality, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular standups and delivery syncs surface progress and blockers; stakeholder updates and risk reviews maintain alignment and support escalation when needed.

Quality practices include unit and applicable integration tests, CI checks for tests and linting, review approval, security scanning, and end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows before release; manual QA is used when appropriate. Releases require acceptance criteria to be met, passing CI and security scans, release notes, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture learnings and track improvement actions.
